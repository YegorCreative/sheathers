Sea Others Glow

Sea Others Glow is a series of albums that were created by Sea Others Foundation. There are three collective community singing albums with 46 original community singing songs and 60 different community singers.

What is Sea Others Foundation (SOF)?

Sea Others Foundation is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt human service non-profit agency that started in 2014.

Sea Others Foundation helps people with referrals and community-building outreach programs...

The goal of the Sea Others Foundation is to help all people find resources...

Thank you for your support. We rely on your donations to keep this non-profit running.

Why we spell Sea Others Foundation instead of See Others Foundation...

Mission Statement

Sea Others Foundation (SOF) is a 501 (c)(3) volunteer, human services non-profit that helps and inspires others with information and creative community outreach programs.

The mission statement for the Sea Others Foundation is to offer a helping hand and inspiration with community involvement.
